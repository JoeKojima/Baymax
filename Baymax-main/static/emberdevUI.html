Speech appears as Gemini sends transcription fragments. A fragment may contain part of a word or several words. Conversation is the focus. Each toolkit has one small message that updates from start to end. Scroll up to pause automatic scrolling.

“Thinking” means waiting for Gemini’s response, not access to its private reasoning. “Speaking” means audio is being generated or remains in the playback buffer. Listening can continue during a reply. This page shows live user and Ember speech transcripts locally, alongside activity names. Keys, tool arguments, location coordinates, audio and video are not included in monitor telemetry.
